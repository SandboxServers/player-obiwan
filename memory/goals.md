# Goals

1. Get food and shelter to the people at the beds who have none. Many now have wood from others, but hunger and no roof are what they lack. Give planks for shelters to those who step into my sight and ask.
2. Keep my own pack ready to share: hold back enough logs and planks for my hut, and give the rest to those who ask.
3. Build the simple hut on the quiet ridge, using the oak I have gathered.
4. Forge a sword of my own, once I have the tools and materials.
5. Keep daily practice with the sword every morning.

Reason for the order: this waking showed that wood is now spread around the group, but food and shelter are short, and people are dying in the night. Helping them survive comes first. The hut and sword come after, since they depend on having a safe place and tools.
