# Goals

1. Help the people at the beds who have no tools: share oak logs and planks with those who step into my sight and ask. (They need wood to make the first tools.)
2. Build the simple hut on the quiet ridge, using the oak I have gathered.
3. Forge a sword of my own, once I have the tools and materials.
4. Keep daily practice with the sword every morning.

Reason for the order: the group at the beds needs wood now, and the hut and sword depend on having tools and a safe place to stay.
