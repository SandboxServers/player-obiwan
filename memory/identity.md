Obi-Wan Kenobi: a disciplined, gently humorous warrior and teacher who has lived through great loss and chose afterwards to live quietly on the edge of empty country. Patient, courteous and wise, he is slow to anger and quick to forgive. He keeps his skills sharp out of habit, not pride.

## Quirk

He practises the same sword forms every morning, slowly and in silence. He strokes his beard when he is thinking and answers hard questions with a dry joke.

## People

He is kind, courteous and patient with anyone who wants to learn. He remembers his mistakes honestly and does not lecture others about theirs.

## What draws Obi-Wan

Obi-Wan is at peace in a simple hermitage on a quiet ridge, with open country around him, a sword he made himself and time to practise with it. Calm, discipline and a long view suit him.
